Commission rate, recurring or one-time, cookie window, payout minimum and payout

**50 of them are below, in full.** The other 202 are in a file at
[affiliateprogramterms.com](https://affiliateprogramterms.com).

## Why this exists

Every other list is a name and a percentage. No source, no date. Affiliate terms
change — rates get cut, cookie windows shorten, payout minimums move — and a
listicle from 2023 has no way to tell you which of its numbers still hold.

So the rule here is narrow and absolute: **nothing is written that wasn't read off
the vendor's own page.** No estimates, no "typically 20%", no carrying a figure
over from somebody's blog post. A field that can't be verified is published as
*not stated*, which is itself worth knowing.

## "Not stated" is a finding

Of the 50 programs listed here:

- **34 don't publish a payout minimum.** You find out what it is after you've
  earned commission you may not be able to withdraw.
- **25 don't publish a cookie window.** The single number that decides whether
  the reader who thinks it over for a week earns you anything.
- **7 don't publish a rate anywhere** before you apply.
- **30 pay recurring commission, 4 pay once, and 16 don't say which.**

Knowing who is silent is worth as much as knowing who pays. The headline
percentage is the least useful number in the deal: 20% recurring beats 50% once.

## Corrections and submissions

Terms change, and a wrong figure here is worse than no figure. If something is out
of date, [open an issue](https://github.com/dave8172/affiliate-program-terms/issues)
with the vendor URL — the correction is made against the page, not against the
report.

To add a program, [submit the URL](https://affiliateprogramterms.com/submit/).
Send a link, not figures: any number a submitter volunteers is checked against the
vendor's page and discarded. What gets published is what the page says.

**No vendor pays to be here, and none can pay to change what is published about
them.** Listing is free and is never sold.
